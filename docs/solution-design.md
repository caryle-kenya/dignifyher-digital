# Solution design

## Demonstration

The current prototype is a local browser experience. A visitor selects one of a few menstrual health topics, chooses a suggested question or types a general question, and sees a prepared educational response. Simple topic matching runs in the browser. If a question does not match, the prototype explains which topics it can cover and encourages a person with a personal concern to speak with a qualified health worker or trusted adult.

## What it does not do

It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, assess symptoms, track cycles, save messages, provide referrals or connect to a health provider. It does not send typed text to a server. The sample English content has not yet been reviewed by Caryle or qualified health reviewers and is not ready for community use.

## Future user journey

1. A user opens an access channel selected after community research.
2. The service explains its educational purpose, privacy behavior and limitations.
3. The user selects an available language and a general topic.
4. The service presents reviewed information with clear uncertainty and source links.
5. Where personal assessment is needed, the service points to an appropriate, verified human care pathway.
6. The user can leave at any point and understand whether any information was retained.

Future language availability, referral routes and safeguarding steps require community and qualified review. No referral partners are confirmed in the supplied project information.
