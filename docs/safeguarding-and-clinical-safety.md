# Safeguarding and clinical safety

DignifyHer Digital is an education concept, not a diagnostic or treatment service. The current browser demonstration shares a small set of general learning notes and directs visitors with personal concerns to a qualified health worker or trusted adult. It has no emergency monitoring or referral connection.

A functioning service would require qualified clinical, community and safeguarding review before use.

## Design requirements to resolve

- Use age-appropriate language and explain the service's limitations.
- Avoid definitive diagnosis, treatment instructions or false reassurance. Make uncertainty visible and direct personal concerns to qualified care.
- Define how urgent or high-risk messages will be handled, including what the service can and cannot monitor.
- Establish consent, safeguarding, confidentiality and escalation procedures suitable for adolescent users before research or pilot activity.
- Do not invite disclosure of sensitive details beyond what is necessary.
- Verify any referral organization, contact details, eligibility and operating hours before publishing them.
- Provide a safe way to report harm or a security concern through a verified channel.

No clinical reviewers, referral partners, escalation procedures or user testing have been confirmed.
