# Project overview

DignifyHer Digital is Caryle Enterprises' proposed digital initiative to improve access to reliable, affordable, confidential and culturally appropriate menstrual and reproductive health information for adolescent girls and women, with an initial focus on underserved communities in Kisumu County, Kenya.

Caryle is a women-led social enterprise based in Kisumu. Its existing work includes DignifyHer reusable sanitary pads and CircularSkills community training. Those initiatives provide context and a community foundation; they are not parts of the digital service.

The concept may combine a guided information experience, a reviewed educational library and signposting to appropriate care or community support. English, Kiswahili and Dholuo are language priorities. WhatsApp and USSD are possible future access channels, subject to research and feasibility.

The repository includes an early browser demonstration with fixed, English-language sample responses. The demo is not a deployed service, has not been clinically reviewed or community-tested, and does not collect or store questions. Translations, referrals, cycle tracking and messaging channels are not included.

The service is intended to complement qualified care, not diagnose or treat. Further work includes community consultation, content review, privacy and safeguarding design, technical development and evaluation.
