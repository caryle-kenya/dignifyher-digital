# Problem statement

Adolescent girls and women in underserved communities in Kenya can face barriers to reliable, affordable, confidential and culturally appropriate menstrual and reproductive health information. The supplied project description identifies stigma, misconceptions, misinformation, language and literacy barriers, limited youth-friendly education, unreliable connectivity and privacy concerns as relevant challenges.

These barriers can make it harder to understand menstrual health, recognise when professional guidance may be appropriate, and find suitable support. The project is proposed in response to a lack of accessible, locally relevant and privacy-conscious digital resources.

The initial geographic focus is Kisumu County. This statement records the project rationale; it does not assert prevalence rates or quantify outcomes. Community research is needed to validate needs, language preferences, access constraints and safe referral pathways before product decisions are made.
