# DignifyHer Digital

**An emerging menstrual health education concept by Caryle Enterprises | Kisumu, Kenya**

DignifyHer Digital is a proposed service for accessible, confidential and culturally relevant menstrual health education for adolescent girls and women. This repository now includes an early, browser-based demonstration of a guided information experience.

## Try the prototype

Open [`prototype/index.html`](prototype/index.html) in a modern browser. It runs locally without an account, server or internet connection. Its reference links require an internet connection.

The demo lets a visitor explore a short set of educational topics, select suggested questions, and type a question to see whether it matches one of those prepared topics. It does not provide personalised assessment, diagnosis or treatment. The typed text stays in the current browser page and is not sent to a server or stored by this demo.

## Status

**Early interface prototype.** The demonstration uses fixed, English-language educational responses and simple topic matching. It is not a deployed service, clinically reviewed product or tested with community participants. Kiswahili and Dholuo content, referrals, cycle tracking, messaging channels and production infrastructure are not included.

The sample information needs review by Caryle and qualified health reviewers before community use. This repository does not claim clinical approval or user testing.

## Safety and privacy

Use fictional questions in this demo. Do not enter a name, contact details or personal health information. If you have a personal health concern, contact a qualified health worker. For severe or worrying symptoms, seek care promptly.

The prototype is an educational demonstration only. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, keep a cycle calendar, or connect to a health provider.
