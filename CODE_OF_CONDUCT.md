# Code of Conduct

This draft is subject to Caryle Enterprises' approval.

We aim to keep project discussions respectful, inclusive and centered on the dignity, privacy and safety of girls and women. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, publication of private information, and stigmatizing language are not acceptable.

If you experience or witness a concern, contact the project maintainers through a verified organisational channel. That channel will be published after approval. Do not include a person's sensitive health information in a public issue.
