# Proposed testing plan

This is a plan only. No technical or user testing results are currently available.

## Before testing

- Define the research questions, prototype version, test tasks and success criteria.
- Obtain project, ethics and safeguarding review as appropriate before recruiting participants, especially adolescents.
- Use informed consent/assent procedures, privacy protections and a safe referral process approved for the activity.
- Use fictional scenarios; do not collect unnecessary identifiable health information.
- Recruit and compensate participants only under an approved plan; document who is eligible and how participation can stop.
- Separate research on Caryle's physical products or training from testing of DignifyHer Digital.

## Suggested evaluation areas

Accessibility and language comprehension; task completion; clarity of limitations; response relevance; harmful or misleading outputs; privacy understanding; low-bandwidth usability; and successful escalation/signposting in defined scenarios.

## Reporting results

After testing, report actual dates, prototype version, participant count and type at a privacy-safe level, methods, findings, limitations and changes made. Do not invent accuracy rates, satisfaction scores or user counts. Keep identifiable information out of this repository.
